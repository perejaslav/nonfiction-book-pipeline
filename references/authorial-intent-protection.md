# Authorial Intent Protection

Do not silently change:
- genre;
- central thesis;
- final conclusion;
- chapter existence;
- major section boundaries;
- interpretive frame.

Safe autonomous changes:
- clarify wording;
- tighten repetition;
- strengthen bridges;
- soften overclaims;
- reorder paragraphs inside a chapter when thesis stays unchanged.

Use this recommendation format for risky changes:

```md
Recommendation:
Why:
Benefit:
Risk:
Needs user approval: yes/no
```
