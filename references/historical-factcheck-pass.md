# Historical fact-check pass (quick/balanced)

Use this pass when a historical nonfiction manuscript is already structurally complete and the goal is to remove the most visible factual risks without turning the text into an overcautious academic apparatus.

## What to check first
- founders/origins of cities and colonies
- exact dates and reign ranges
- named rulers and dynasties
- quantitative claims (grain, army size, area, distances)
- absolute formulations: "first", "unique", "main", "most important", "proved", "certainly"

## Working rule
- If a claim is widely accepted and not controversial, keep it.
- If a claim is plausible but not central, soften it with hedging.
- If a claim is wrong or unsupported, correct it explicitly.
- Prefer "по античной традиции", "по-видимому", "вероятно", "по оценкам", "судя по археологии" over certainty markers.

## Session-derived corrections to remember
- Apollonia Pontica should be described as founded by Milesian colonists, not Samians.
- Large grain-export figures for the Bosporan Kingdom are better rendered as "significant volume" unless a source-anchored estimate is available.
- The Chersonesus chora is better described as one of the best-studied systems in the Black Sea region, rather than as uniquely unparalleled.

## Safe publication stance
A manuscript is "publication-ready" only after the quick fact-check removes obvious errors and the remaining uncertainty is marked in the prose rather than hidden.
