# Literary Critique Checklist for Publication Readiness

Use this when the user asks for a manuscript review “as a literary critic” / “as a literary editor”.

## Workflow
1. Read opening, middle, and ending separately.
2. Scan for repeated phrases and repeated ideas before writing prose feedback.
3. Check heading hierarchy and any editor/working markers that should not survive to publication.
4. Distinguish:
   - strong core concept;
   - repeat-heavy passages;
   - over-explained passages;
   - structural blockers;
   - minor copyedits.
5. End with a prioritized repair plan, not just observations.

## Fast repetition audit
Look especially for:
- "не просто X, а Y"
- "это не X. Это Y"
- "важно понимать"
- "по сути"
- "именно"
- "царь царей"
- repeated named lists / catalogues
- repeated comparison pairs like камень/глина, идеал/реальность, центр/периферия

## What to report
- opening hook strength
- over-explanation / lecture tone
- duplicated ideas across nearby sections
- predictable paragraph endings
- overuse of qualifiers and metacommentary
- ending density: one strong close vs multiple weaker closes
- structural issues in headings / levels / service labels

## Output shape
- short verdict
- bullet list of weak spots with examples
- prioritized edit plan:
  1. blockers
  2. deduplication
  3. style cooling
  4. line edits

Keep it concise but specific. The goal is publication readiness, not a general summary.
