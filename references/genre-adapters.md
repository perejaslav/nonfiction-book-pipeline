# Genre Adapters

## Historical nonfiction
Use the full historical extension set: `entities.md`, `fact_risk_map.md`, `reconstruction_policy.md`, evidence packs, and balanced fact-check before publication.

## Biography
Add a life chronology map, separate verified facts from later legend, and keep era context attached to each major life turn.

## Business / practical nonfiction
Prioritize reader pain points, frameworks, case studies, applicability, and anti-overclaiming discipline. Evidence packs should focus on statistics, market context, and case credibility.

## Textbook / guide
Add learning sequence, recap logic, exercises or control questions when requested, and stronger terminology/glossary discipline.

## Essayistic / hybrid nonfiction
Use a lighter evidence-pack posture when claims are interpretive rather than empirical, but strengthen bridge logic and voice consistency.
